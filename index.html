The plan

Drive in through the south, two nights at Skukuza, two at Satara, then out through Orpen Gate. One move, no backtracking, two very different parts of the park.

We camp in SANParks rest camps, cook our own food, and book guided drives for the best hours: sunset, night and sunrise. The rest of the time we self-drive the park, which costs nothing extra.

Aiming for the September university recess in 2027 (16 to 26 September), with the July recess as backup. Both are in the dry season: the bush is thinner, animals stay near water, nights are cool and malaria risk is lower.

Take turns

Early starts

Drivers swap

Before we go

  • Medical and next of kinSend me your allergies, medication and an emergency contact before we leave.
  • Malaria checkKruger is a malaria area. Talk to a pharmacy or doctor a few weeks before.
  • First aidThere'll be a proper first aid kit in the car.
  • Park rulesStay in vehicles outside camps, and be through the gate before it closes. Late arrivals get fined.
  • Lock up the foodCamps are fenced, but monkeys, baboons and honey badgers raid tents and cooler boxes. Food goes in cars at night.

    Questions

    Is it safe to sleep in a tent?

    Yes. Rest camps are fenced and staffed. The real risk is monkeys and honey badgers going after food, so food stays locked in the car.

    I don't have camping gear.

    Tell me. Between us we can usually borrow what's missing before anyone has to hire.

    Can I bring a friend?

    Please do. Every extra person makes it cheaper for everyone, up to two full cars.

    What if I can only do a few days?

    Message me. If you have your own car, you can join for part of it.

    Is there a student discount?

    Not at SANParks, sadly. The best saving is a Wild Card: R895 for a year of free entry to every national park. On the 4-night trip it saves about R700 in conservation fees, and if you come to Kgalagadi in 2028 too, it pays for itself twice over.

    Any other ways to save?

    SANParks sometimes drops campsite prices by 20 to 50% at short notice, mostly midweek at quieter camps. If one lands on our dates, the savings get split back to everyone.

    Do I need malaria pills?

    Kruger is a malaria area. Ask a pharmacy or doctor a few weeks before.
